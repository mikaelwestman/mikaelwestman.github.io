I'm Mikael Westman. A designer from Sweden. After a decade in tech, building software in New York and Stockholm, I've stepped away from corporate offices and into the workshop.

For the last two years, my practice has lived in the physical: furniture, lamps, and objects for the home. Trading a traditional design process for a method of making based around intuition, serendipity, and play.

Write me at mikael@wst.mn or connect on Linkedin.
